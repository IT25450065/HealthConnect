❓ Module Help & Operational Guidance

Click any question below to expand the operational guidelines for this module.

Go to the Stock Management tab and enter a medicine name or code (e.g. MED-1002). If the medicine exists, click "Receive Delivery Stock" to add received batch quantities and supplier reference codes. If it does not exist, click "Add New Medicine Record" to specify item code, unit, minimum threshold, and initial stock.
Every time inventory changes (after stock deliveries or prescription dispensing), the server checks if the updated quantity is at or below the configured min_threshold. If true, an alert is raised, highlighted on the dashboard alert panel with a pulsing indicator, and flagged in red/amber.
Open the Low/Out Stock List tab. Use the filter buttons to switch between All Alerts, Low Stock Only, and Out of Stock Only. Use the sort dropdown to order items by Deficit Gap Amount, Medicine Name (A-Z), or Stock Level.
On the Dispensing Queue tab, doctor-issued prescriptions are listed with patient details and prescribed medicine lines. Clicking "Dispense Line Item" or "Dispense All Items" automatically deducts the prescribed quantity from medicine inventory. If inventory stock is insufficient, dispensing is blocked with a clear warning to prevent negative stock levels.
